# KnowYourGrub — stand loop

A single-file looping presentation for the National AI Challenge 2026 final stand.
Open `index.html` in a browser and press **F** for fullscreen. Tap or press any key to
pause and show the controls; the loop resumes on its own after 25 seconds.

## Files

- `index.html` — the whole thing. Settings are in the `CFG` block at the top of the script.
- `assets/` — optional. Drop in:
  - `kyg.mp4` (or `kyg.webm`) — the video. Plays muted in full; skipped if missing.
  - `logo-horizontal.png`, `logo.png`, `grub.png`, `app-qr.png`, `app-scan.png`,
    `pro-overview.png`, `kilowen.png`, `greentechhq.png` — copies of the images from
    knowyourgrub.com/assets. Without them the page loads the images from the site,
    which needs wifi at the venue.

## Controls

Tap / click — pause and show controls · ← → — previous / next · **P** — pause/resume ·
**F** — fullscreen · **M** — video sound on/off · swipe on a tablet.
