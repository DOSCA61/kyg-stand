The part nobody tells you

The label is allowed not to tell you.

Under EU Regulation 1169/2011, wheat‑based glucose syrup need not mention wheat. “Lecithin” may be sunflower — or soya. We checked nine label apps; none handles these legal omissions.

Ingredients: Wholegrain oats, glucose syrup, milk solids, natural flavouring, emulsifier (lecithin), malt extract…

KnowYourGrub: the label says milk and malt. It does not say what the glucose syrup was made from, and the law does not require it to. Here is why that matters for you.

Our promise

We don’t tell you whether food is safe.

We tell you what the label says, what it doesn’t, and why the difference matters. The choice — and the confidence — stays with you.

Built with AI — responsibly

The model is never allowed to assert.

Grounding

Every answer comes from the label

The AI only phrases what is already in the product’s own facts. It cannot add a number, resolve an ambiguity, or claim something is absent.

Ambiguity knowledge base

18 legal gaps, each cited to the law

What a label is permitted to leave out — lecithin, E471, glucose syrup, gelatine, “may contain” — written down once and checked against.

Fails closed

An answer that goes too far is thrown away

Not softened. The label’s own words go up instead. Seventy‑seven self‑checking features verify the build on every launch.

Recall watch
